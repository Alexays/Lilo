## [0.19.0] - 2026-11-08

### Added

- Right-clicking an image offers Search Image with Google Lens, or with Bing when it is your search engine

### Fixed

- Holding ⌘ over a terminal line of wide characters, such as Chinese text or emoji, no longer freezes Lilo
- Every color picked for legibility follows perceived contrast, as the sidebar does: on a medium blue or red Space, terminal colors keep their hue instead of all turning white, and in dark mode secondary text, error messages and links read more clearly.
- A call keeps sending your camera while you are on another tab, even with automatic picture in picture turned off
- The Downloads and Archived headers in the sidebar menu no longer run into their actions in longer languages: clear and show all are now icons
- Extensions without the tabs permission learn a new tab's address when it starts loading, instead of sometimes missing it
- Pressing Enter in a page's search field no longer plays the system alert sound
