## [0.15.1] - 2026-11-04

### Changed

- Settings draws each address placement as a small window, like the tab positions, showing where the address goes with the tabs where they are
- The active Space's tile in the sidebar wears its profile color as a soft tint instead of a bright pastel
- With the tabs at the bottom, their bar mirrors the top one, along the window's bottom edge instead of floating as a dock
- The sidebar toggle and the back, forward and reload buttons share one capsule, in the tab strip as above the page, and the strip's Space button matches their size
