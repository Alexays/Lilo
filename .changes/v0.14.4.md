## [0.14.4] - 2026-11-03

### Changed

- Settings, the About panel and bug reports show which WebKit Lilo runs on: the one of macOS, or Lilo's build with engine fixes ahead of macOS
- When a secure connection fails before the site presents a certificate, the error page now says so and gives macOS's reason instead of blaming the certificate, and an error reached through a redirect names the address that was opened
- On a Glass Space, the address field, pinned tiles, open folders and the selected tab now let the glass show through
- The traffic lights line up with the left edge of the address bar, leaving the tabs header a little more room

### Fixed

- Two sites on one host but different ports, such as two local servers, now each show their own favicon.
- Opening the Spaces menu during the very first launch no longer crashes Lilo
- After dragging the window by the bar over the page, the pointer works on the page again instead of staying on the toolbar
- The Settings sidebar no longer stays shifted sideways, cut off on the left, after it opens
- The top of Settings and the other side panels now moves the window, like the top of the sidebar
- The Grain material now covers the whole window as one seamless texture, with no edge between the sidebar and the frame
