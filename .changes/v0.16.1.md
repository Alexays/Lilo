## [0.16.1] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- Toolbar and tab icons hint at their action on hover: reload turns, arrows nudge, close and new tab rotate, and the Agents mascot blinks
- An open folder's or stack's own tabs sit at the block's edge instead of a step in, leaving more room for their titles
- Agent tabs render at 60 fps even with Full refresh rate on, saving battery while agents work.
- Agent tabs nobody is looking at stop drawing while the agent thinks, and wake for its next action.
- Tabs scrolling under the pinned divider fade out instead of being cut off at its edge.
- Switching tabs updates the address bar faster.

### Fixed

- History and archived tabs in the command bar show their site's icon even when no tab of that site is open.
- A call page answering late no longer opens its picture-in-picture window after you come back to its tab.
- Sidebar and tab strip menus open out of their button and fade away when they close, instead of vanishing at once
- Agents sharing one connection, such as Claude Desktop's chats, each get their own tab folder for the task they name, instead of all working in one
- The agent's performance profile no longer counts overlapping long tasks twice, and shows the main thread's CPU for each long task, so a record WebKit left open no longer reads as a frozen page
- The agent's performance profile of a page that moved to a new process no longer mixes in timings of the page it left
