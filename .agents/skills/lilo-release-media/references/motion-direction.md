# Motion direction

The supplied Linear references show three useful forms. They are visual observations, not claims about Linear's implementation.

- [Environment panels](https://webassets.linear.app/files/ornj730p/production/3922f252288414570e31823dbfc947723a2e59a2.mp4): related cards move through a continuous perspective composition. Overlap, parallax and selective peripheral focus carry attention between them. Use for a native panel showcase.
- [Workflow dial](https://webassets.linear.app/files/ornj730p/production/80f59b063433c661b3aeae3f613f0cf2a58d3a35.mp4): a travelling connection links trigger and result. Sparse graphics explain a relationship. Use only when that relationship is real and useful to understand.
- [Conversation](https://webassets.linear.app/files/ornj730p/production/9e1278d821662e7bea1b814dc3cbb89f28671943.mp4): a stable frontal interaction becomes successive results. Short native clips suit typing and actual state changes; still layers help compose the surrounding sequence.

Establish one subject, show the meaningful change, then hold a useful result. Carry position, camera direction and focus into the next beat instead of restarting unrelated movements. Use depth deliberately for a showcase; settle into a sharp frontal view while the viewer reads or follows interaction. Blur peripheral material only. Remove idle time and wandering pointer travel while preserving causality.

For example, the 0.8.0 movie focuses on finding a tab and switching the shared source. It does not also need to demonstrate stopping the call. Keep a short coherent benefit story instead of copying every checkpoint from the longer source capture.

Crop to the relevant real panel or control. Browser furniture earns its place by explaining the feature. Test readability at the actual compact card width, including mobile. Avoid baked marketing titles that duplicate the translated surrounding text, long logo closes and blank/logo posters.

The references last 17–30 seconds. Choose duration from the story and reading time, without a universal 15-second ceiling. A concise changelog story may be shorter. Do not fill time with decorative movement.
