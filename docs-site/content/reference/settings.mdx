The rows of Settings, in the order Settings shows them, with the text the row itself shows. The lists and cards Settings shows between rows, such as your Spaces and profiles, your installed extensions or the app tiles under **Apps that can use Lilo**, are not repeated here. Open Settings with **Lilo > Settings…** (<kbd>⌘,</kbd>), or type in **Search settings** to find a row by name.

How to read the tables:

- Sections and groups follow the Settings sidebar and the headings inside each section.
- **Default** is the position of an on/off switch on a new installation. Rows that open a list, a menu or a page leave it empty: what they start with depends on your Mac or is shown in the row. **Install updates automatically** also shows no default: it is on in a release you install, and a build from source does not update itself.
- Your answer to the setup's **How will you use Lilo?** changes two defaults: **With my agents** and **For developing** turn **Let agents use Lilo** on, and only **For developing** leaves **Terminal tabs** on.
- Some rows appear only while the switch above them is on: the rows after **Block ads and trackers** need it on, for example, and so do the rows after **Let agents use Lilo**.
- The **Terminal** section is described in [Terminal](/guides/terminal).
