Find your symptom below and follow its fix. If none helps, collect diagnostics and [report the bug](#report-a-bug).

## A site does not work as in another browser

Lilo runs on WebKit, the engine of Safari, and sites see it as Safari. A site that breaks in Lilo often breaks in Safari too. Try these in order:

1. **Reload without the content blocker.** Click the shield in the address bar (**Disable blocking on this site**), then reload the page (<kbd>⌘R</kbd>). The site is then listed in **Settings > Privacy > Sites without blocking**. To turn blocking back on, click the shield again (**Re-enable blocking on this site**).
2. **Reload without the cache**: <kbd>⇧⌘R</kbd>.
3. **Check the site's permissions.** **Settings > Privacy > Settings by site** shows the permissions, zoom and stored data of each site.
4. **Embedded sign-ins and widgets.** If an embedded site you use stops working (a sign-in in a frame, a comment widget), turn off **Prevent cross-site tracking** in **Settings > Privacy**, then turn it back on when you are done.
5. **Check the known limits.** Some features cannot work in any WebKit browser other than Safari: Web Push, Apple Pay on the web, and the iCloud Passwords extension among them. [Web compatibility](/reference/web-compatibility) lists each one with its cause.

More about these controls in [Privacy controls](/guides/privacy-controls).

## A page stops responding

When a page freezes, Lilo shows "example.com isn't responding" over it. Your other tabs keep working. Choose **Wait**, **Reload** or **Close Tab**.

If the same site freezes again, the card offers **Reload Without Blocker**: on some sites the content blocker causes the freeze. **Report a problem** opens a bug report about the frozen page, with only its site's address in it.

## A page cannot be loaded

The error page names the problem, such as **Server Not Found**, **Connection Timed Out** or **No Internet Connection**, and offers **Try Again**. When the connection fails or you are offline, **Show Saved Copy** shows the copy of the page in Lilo's cache, if it has one.

**Connection Not Secure** means the site's certificate is invalid or expired. Go **Back** unless you know why the certificate fails.

## An extension does not work

1. Check [Extension compatibility](/reference/extension-compatibility): it lists what differs from Chrome and the extensions tested, with their results. Manifest V2 extensions do not run.
2. Open the extension in **Settings > Extensions** and check that it is **Enabled** and that **Site access** includes the site. On a private Space, it needs **Allow in private Spaces**.
3. If its details read "Couldn't load this extension.", click **Retry**. If they read "Background stopped: some features may not work.", turn it off and on again with **Enabled**, or quit and reopen Lilo.
4. Open **Help > Diagnostics** and choose **Log**, then **Extensions**, to see the extension's errors. **Copy** copies them for a report.

To report an extension that fails, use the extension compatibility form in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Alexays/Lilo/issues/new/choose).

## Lilo does not update

Lilo checks for updates on its own every hour and, with **Install updates automatically** on (**Settings > General**), downloads them in the background. When an update is ready, the sidebar shows **Ready to install**: click **Restart** to install it now, or **Later**.

- To check now, choose **Lilo > Check for Updates…**, or click **Check Now** in **Settings > General**, next to your Lilo version.
- If Lilo runs from the disk image, it cannot update itself. Lilo offers to move itself to Applications ("Move Lilo to Applications?"): choose **Move to Applications**. If the move fails, drag Lilo to the Applications folder in the Finder, then open it from there.
- If **Settings > General** reads "This build does not update itself.", you are running a build that does not update itself, such as one built from source. Install the release from [Install](/start/install).

## An agent cannot connect

1. **Turn on agent control.** It is off in a new profile. In **Settings > Agents**, turn on **Let agents use Lilo**. Until you do, every tool call fails with `[needs_user] Lilo is not accepting agents: in Lilo, turn on Settings > AI agents > Let agents use Lilo, then reconnect the lilo MCP server.` (the Settings section is named **Agents**).
2. **Reconnect the server.** After turning it on, restart your agent or reconnect its `lilo` MCP server: an agent started before keeps the earlier answer.
3. **Answer the prompt.** The first connection waits for you to choose **Allow** in Lilo. If you chose **Don't Allow**, reconnect the server to be asked again.
4. **Check where Lilo is.** Agents start `/Applications/Lilo.app/Contents/MacOS/lilo-mcp`. If you moved Lilo, Lilo fixes the configuration of Claude Desktop, Cursor and Codex at its next launch. For Claude Code, click **Connect** again in **Settings > Agents**.
5. **Lilo not responding.** If the agent reports `Lilo is not responding`, quit and reopen Lilo, then reconnect.

The full setup is in [AI agents](/guides/ai-agents).

## Lilo feels slow or uses too much memory

- **Help > Diagnostics** shows **Startup**, how long launch took with the slowest step highlighted, and **Memory**, how many pages are loaded and sleeping. **Run Trim** applies the automatic sleep policy now.
- **Process manager**, in **Settings > Advanced** under **Developer tools**, shows the memory and CPU used by each tab and process.
- **Help > Record Performance Session** records what Lilo does, for up to 10 minutes, until you choose it again. The recording is saved to your Downloads folder, ready to attach to a report. Nothing is recorded outside a session.

## Report a bug

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search first">
    Look through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Alexays/Lilo/issues) for the same problem. For a question rather than a bug, ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Alexays/Lilo/discussions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the diagnostics">
    Open **Help > Diagnostics** and choose **Copy Report**. It copies the startup metrics and tab lifecycle counts. For an extension, also copy its log from **Log**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Choose **Help > Report an Issue…**. Lilo opens the bug report form with your Lilo, WebKit and macOS versions filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Give the steps that reproduce the problem, paste the report, and send it. Replace real addresses and names with made-up ones such as `example.com`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Never report a security vulnerability in public: follow the [security policy](https://github.com/Alexays/Lilo/blob/main/SECURITY.md) to report it privately.
