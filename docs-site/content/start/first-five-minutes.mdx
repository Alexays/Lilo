This page finishes the setup, then walks you through six moves: open a page from the command bar, pin a tab, peek at a link, group tabs in a folder, put two pages side by side, and make a second Space. Do them in order, in your own window; each takes under a minute.

## Finish the setup

After the import, the setup has one or two screens left.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your Lilo">
    Choose where your **Tabs** go (**Left**, **Right**, **Top** or **Bottom**), where the **Address** shows, the **Theme**, and the **Color** and **Material** of your first Space. The window behind the setup changes as you pick.

    Under **Essentials**, leave **Block ads and trackers** on, and click **Use Lilo** next to **Open links in Lilo** if you want links from your mail and other apps to open here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your agents">
    This screen shows only if you answered **With my agents** or **For developing**. Leave **Let agents use Lilo** on, then click **Connect** next to each app you want to use with Lilo. [AI agents](/guides/ai-agents) explains what they can do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let's Go">
    Click **Let's Go**. Lilo says **Welcome to Lilo.** If the tab in front is blank, the command bar opens over it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The rest of this page assumes your tabs are on the left, the default. The sidebar shows your Space's tabs: favorites as tiles at the top, then pinned tabs, then the tabs you open.

## 1. Open a page from the command bar

The command bar is how you open, find and do almost anything in Lilo.

1. Press <kbd>⌘T</kbd>. A new tab opens with the command bar over it.
2. Type `example.com` and press <kbd>Return</kbd>.
3. Press <kbd>⌘T</kbd> again and type a few letters of a page you visited. Your open tabs, history and bookmarks come up with the web search, and choosing an open tab switches to it.

Start with `@` to search only open tabs, `/` for history, `#` for bookmarks or `:` for actions such as **New Split View**. <kbd>⌘L</kbd> opens the command bar on the current page's address, to edit it. More in [The command bar](/guides/command-bar).

## 2. Pin a tab

Tabs you open are temporary: a tab left alone for 12 hours moves to the archive, so the sidebar stays short. Pin the tabs you come back to.

1. Right-click the `example.com` tab in the sidebar and choose **Pin Tab**. It moves up with your pinned tabs.
2. Quit Lilo with <kbd>⌘Q</kbd> and open it again: the pinned tab is still there.

You can also drag a tab up to the pinned tabs. To get an archived tab back, press <kbd>⌘T</kbd> and start with `<`. Each Space sets its own delay; see [Tabs and folders](/guides/tabs-and-folders).

## 3. Peek at a link

A pinned tab keeps its place: a link from it to another site opens in Peek, over the page, instead of taking the tab away.

1. In your pinned `example.com` tab, click the link on the page: it leads to another site, so it opens in Peek over the current page.
2. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or click outside it, to close it. You are back where you were.
3. In any tab, hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> and click a link to peek at it. To keep the page, click **Open as Tab** at the top of the Peek, or press <kbd>⌘O</kbd>.

See [Peek and Little window](/guides/peek-and-little-window).

## 4. Group tabs in a folder

1. Open two or three pages on the same topic.
2. Right-click one of them in the sidebar and choose **Create Folder from Tab**.
3. Drag the other tabs onto the folder. Click the folder to close or open it.

Right-click the folder and choose **Edit Folder** to rename it and pick a color. Folders nest, and can be locked; see [Tabs and folders](/guides/tabs-and-folders).

## 5. Put two pages side by side

1. Open a second tab, so there is one to show and one to drag.
2. Drag a tab from the sidebar onto the right edge of the page, and drop it on **Open on the right**.
3. Both pages now share the window. Drag the line between them to resize.

A split view holds up to four tabs. To undo it, right-click one of its tabs and choose **Separate Split View**. More in [Split view](/guides/split-view).

## 6. Make a second Space

A Space is a set of tabs with its own name, emoji and color: one for work, one for home. Your first Space is called **Home**.

1. Click **New Space**, the plus button at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Pick an emoji, type a name, and click **Create Space**. Lilo switches to the new, empty Space.
3. Click the Space icons at the bottom of the sidebar to switch, or press <kbd>⌃1</kbd> and <kbd>⌃2</kbd>.

<kbd>⌃⌘→</kbd> and <kbd>⌃⌘←</kbd> go to the next and previous Space. Under **Signed in as**, a Space can share your sign-ins or keep its own; see [Spaces and profiles](/guides/spaces-and-profiles).

## Shortcuts to remember

| Shortcut | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>⌘T</kbd> | New tab, with the command bar |
| <kbd>⌘L</kbd> | Edit the current address |
| <kbd>⌘W</kbd> | Close the tab |
| <kbd>⇧⌘T</kbd> | Reopen the last closed tab |
| <kbd>⌃Tab</kbd> | Switch to your most recent tab |
| <kbd>⌘1</kbd> to <kbd>⌘8</kbd> | Go to a tab by its place in the sidebar; <kbd>⌘9</kbd> goes to the last one |
| <kbd>⇧⌘S</kbd> | Hide or show the sidebar |
| <kbd>⌘,</kbd> | Settings |
| <kbd>⌘/</kbd> | Every keyboard shortcut |

Coming from Arc? Open **Settings** > **General** > **Keyboard shortcuts** and set **Preset** to **Arc**. The full list is in [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts).

To see the setup again, open **Settings** > **Advanced** and click **Replay** next to **Replay the welcome tour**.
